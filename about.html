About Goibniu

We help laboratories accomplish more with less hardware investment

Goibniu develops software solutions that transform ordinary equipment and minimal hardware setups into practical, capable, low-cost laboratory tools. We provide software on a pay once, use forever basis and provide guidance on getting your workflow set up.

Our mission

Accessible science through intelligent software

We believe capability shouldn’t be gated behind expensive instruments. By putting intelligence in software, Goibniu makes laboratory automation and scientific workflows accessible to teams of every size and budget.

From independent researchers to established R&D groups, our goal is the same: remove cost and complexity as barriers to good science. We also seek to empower scientists at all levels, providing tools that maximise productivity. For example, VoxPage is an audiobook maker that can convert everything from your night time reading to literature reviews into an immersive audio experience. This tool was inspired to help those that wish to maximise their commute, exercise time, or simply remove the fatigue associated with excessive reading in order to take your learning (and leisure!) to a new level.

Inspiration

Practical innovation, forged from need

Goibniu is named for the Irish god of craftsmanship and smithing — a fitting emblem for a company that forges practical tools. We focus on real problems and pragmatic solutions rather than novelty for its own sake.
